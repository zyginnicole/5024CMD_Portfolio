Limitations

The rules come from 10 deliberately chosen records (7 Paid, 3 Not_Paid), so they illustrate the technique and are not a lending tool. No rule predicts Not_Paid: the three Not_Paid loans share no value on any attribute, and class-association rules on the 50-record sample (min. support 0.05, min. confidence 0.5) also found none. The checker therefore returns only “Paid” or “no rule matched”, and never flags a likely default. Lift is a ceiling set by the 70/30 subset (1.43), not evidence of strength; in the full data the two applied rules have lift of about 1.0. Rules on high dti and medium interest rate are not applied because the full data contradicts them. The Medium-FICO rule is applied but is marginal: its full-data Paid rate with these cut-offs (83.5%) is slightly below the 84.0% base rate.
